# KEY ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT RISK — MEMO TO CEO
**Date:** 05 June 2026
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team
**Subject:** Over-concentration of accounts under a single sales representative (Peter)

---

## Summary

An analysis of the customer database (2,273 records) has identified a significant
account management risk. Sales Representative **Peter** currently manages **209 customers
across 22 separate regions** — the widest geographical spread of any rep in the business.

---

## The Risk

| Risk Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| Total customers managed | 209 |
| Number of regions covered | 22 |
| Share of total customer base | 9.2% |
| Primary region (Ibadan / Oyo) | 115 customers (55% of Peter's portfolio) |
| Regions with only 1 customer | 13 out of 22 regions |

Peter is the **second-largest rep by customer count** and the **most geographically
spread rep in the entire business.** This creates three specific risks:

1. **Single point of failure.** If Peter leaves, is unavailable, or resigns,
   209 customer relationships — spread across 22 regions — have no clear successor.
   Transitioning this portfolio would be disruptive and time-consuming.

2. **Shallow coverage in 13 regions.** Peter holds just 1 customer in 13 of his
   22 regions (e.g., Kaduna, Enugu, Ghana, Benue State, Uyo). These customers
   likely receive infrequent attention and are at higher risk of churn.

3. **Overload risk.** Managing 209 accounts across 22 regions — including
   geographically distant ones like Ghana and Uyo — is not sustainable for
   one individual and may already be affecting service quality.

---

## Recommended Actions for CEO Consideration

1. **Conduct an account audit** on Peter's 209 customers to assess relationship
   quality and identify any at-risk accounts.

2. **Reassign the 13 single-customer outlier regions** to the nearest active
   rep with capacity (e.g., the Kaduna customer could move to Stephen or
   Stephen Olushola who already cover that region).

3. **Define clear regional boundaries** for each rep to prevent continued
   overlap and ensure every customer has a clearly responsible rep.

4. **Create a succession or backup plan** for Peter's top accounts, particularly
   his 115 Ibadan/Oyo customers, which represent over half his portfolio.
